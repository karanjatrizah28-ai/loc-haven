A little about Loc Haven

Care that starts at the root.

Loc Haven is a space to slow down, ask questions, and feel at home in your hair. We believe a loc journey should be personal: shaped by your lifestyle, your goals, and the way you want to feel in your own skin.

From the first conversation through your ongoing care, each appointment is grounded in listening, education, and respect for your hair's natural story.

Good to know.

Do I need to know everything before I reach out?

Not at all. A consultation is a space to ask questions, talk through your goals, and understand what the process could look like for you.

How do I know which service is right for me?

Tell us a little about your hair and what you're hoping for in the inquiry form. We can help you find a useful place to begin.

What should I include in my first inquiry?

Your name, the service you're curious about, and any questions or timing preferences are a lovely start. You don't need to have a whole plan yet.
